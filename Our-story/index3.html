OUR FOUNDATION

BUILT ON THREE PILLARS

Wood-Fire Mastery

We believe in primal cooking techniques using natural hardwood charcoal and smoky ember grates that unlock deep, unadulterated flavors in every cut.

Ethical & Local Sourcing

From hand-selected spices for our signature Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani to premium cuts used in our Ribeye Steak, 90%+ of our ingredients are locally sourced daily.

Authentic Hospitality

Elevated dining with a warm, inviting soul. We welcome every guest as a personal friend into our kitchen family for an unforgettable meal.

CHRONICLES

OUR HISTORY & MILESTONES

2018

The Spark in Bandra

Ember Kitchen opened its doors as an intimate 30-seat boutique grill house in Mumbai, crafted around Executive Chef Vikram Saluja's obsession with open-flame cooking.

2020

Perfecting the Crafts

Earned "Best Flame Grill House" accolades while perfecting signature recipes like our slow-charred Tandoori Specialties and artisanal Wood-Fired Spicy Chicken Pizza.

2022

Expanding across India

Brought our open-flame culinary experience to Koramangala (Bengaluru) and Connaught Place (Delhi), introducing region-inspired specialties alongside our signature steak house menu.

2024 - Present

Over 500,000 Plates Served

Honored among India's top fine dining destinations, serving half a million satisfied food enthusiasts while maintaining an incredible 4.8-star guest rating.
